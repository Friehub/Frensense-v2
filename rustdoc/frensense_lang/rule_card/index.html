Module rule_card 

Source
Expand description

Rule cards: the one place a finding’s identity, title, CWE and advice live. Terminal, SARIF, JSON and explain all render from here, so the surfaces cannot drift and a built-in rule cannot ship without a CWE (see the coverage tests here and in frensense-packgen).

Taint findings resolve a card from the sink label slug (SinkLabel::slug); non-dataflow checkers resolve one from the checker rule id. Bundle-learned and user policy rules are not listed: they carry their own prose, and their CWE comes from the bundle pattern overlay.
