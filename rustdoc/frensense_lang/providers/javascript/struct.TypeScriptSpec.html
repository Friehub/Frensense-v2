fn for_loop_iterable<'t>(&self, node: Node<'t>) -> Option<(Node<'t>, Node<'t>)>

JS/TS for … in and for … of loops expose the iterable via the right field and the loop binding via the left field.

A for (x in obj) or for (x of iterable) has no meaningful runtime iteration sink matching the Python model, but emitting the iterable as IR still ensures taint from the right expression reaches the loop body, which is the conservative-and-correct behaviour.
