pub fn extract_facts(families: &[Family]) -> (FactTable, Vec<LearnedFact>)
Expand description

Full extraction: propose per family -> replay-gate -> merged learned table.

Production path: every family is proposed, baselined and gate-trialed under exactly the language specs of its own files - the same tables the consumer CLI builds for those files (family_tables). Harness/CLI parity matters in both directions: a foreign-language rule must not silence a positive during learning, and a foreign-language sanitizer must not quiet a negative the CLI would flag.
