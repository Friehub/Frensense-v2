Engine output that becomes an advisory: a vulnerable verdict on a dangerous sink slot (sink-signature aware), whose sink names a vulnerability class.

alert is None exactly when the slot is a safe channel - the engine already evaluated per-slot danger (jwt.verify(token, secret) slot 1 = secret, query(sql, params) slot 1+ = binding channel). A BackwardVerdict::Vulnerable verdict on a non-dangerous slot must not become an advisory.

The class comes from the rule card the finding reports under (see frensense_lang::rule_card). A sink whose label is unknown and whose role pins no class resolves to a fallback card (“untrusted data reaches a sensitive operation”), which tells the reader only that tainted data went somewhere: nothing to fix, suppress or trend. Those findings are dropped here, so no surface (CLI, MCP, LSP, SARIF) reports them and --explain lists the sink as silent instead.
