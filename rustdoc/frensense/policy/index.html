Reporting policy: which engine findings become advisories, which survive the severity/confidence floor (ENGINE_PURITY_REFACTOR 1.3), and what the report may claim the scan actually covered (Coverage).

The engine emits verdicts; what becomes a reported advisory is consumer policy. This module is the only place that answers

  1. which engine findings become advisories (reports_finding),
  2. which advisories survive the severity/confidence floor (apply), and
  3. how much of the input the scan really inspected (Coverage).

crate::reporter renders what survives; nothing else filters. The --explain “why the engine stayed silent” map lives in crate::explain.

Functions§

apply
Apply the reporting floor: drop advisories below min_confidence or under the severity floor (None = every tier; Some(Severity::Info) is the no-op floor, so tool surfaces that default to "info" pass everything through). Uses Profile::Default.
apply_with_profile
Apply the reporting floor and active scan profile: drop advisories below min_confidence, under severity_floor, or excluded by profile. Returns the surviving advisories and the count of hidden findings by tier.
apply_with_profile_and_hygiene
Apply the reporting floor, active scan profile, and optional hygiene flag: drop advisories below min_confidence, under severity_floor, or excluded by profile (unless enable_hygiene is true and a.tier == RuleTier::Hygiene). Returns the surviving advisories and the count of hidden findings by tier.
apply_with_profile_and_hygiene_counted
Apply the reporting policy and also count findings dropped by confidence and severity floors. A finding is assigned to the first gate that drops it.
reports_finding
Engine output that becomes an advisory: a vulnerable verdict on a dangerous sink slot (sink-signature aware), whose sink names a vulnerability class.
severity_from_label
Parse a severity threshold label strictly, from the one shared six-label vocabulary (frensense_lang::severity::SEVERITY_LABELS).
